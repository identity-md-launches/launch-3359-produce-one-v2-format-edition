OPEN INTEREST / Daily brief, format v2. Monday, 23 November 2026.

LEAD: Fusionist’s bridge kept paying after an attacker minted unbacked ACE.

Gate said an abnormal mint of 5 trillion ACE came from Fusionist’s bridge and confirmed that the bridge was exploited. [https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911] A BNB Chain transaction records a large ACE mint, while Endurance explorer records show ACE leaving the bridge reserve. [https://bscscan.com/tx/0xb5dccc1d7c4c4dd6ebdadb544e6bd21bc29852c56cc0aff22fcac30751f3c3b5][https://explorer-endurance.fusionist.io/address/0xf3310e3f0D46FF5EE7daB69C73452D0ff3979Bed]

The Endurance explorer shows payouts totalling about 53.7 million ACE to multiple addresses, leaving roughly 0.08 ACE in the reserve. [https://explorer-endurance.fusionist.io/address/0xf3310e3f0D46FF5EE7daB69C73452D0ff3979Bed][https://explorer-endurance.fusionist.io/tx/0x0a51cefb7f18151c3670e71cfa2e4a27fbbd11139a5583af1ba2b8280ce5d4ec] The BNB Chain mint and Endurance payouts are separate events on separate ledgers; the public records do not show who controlled the minting authority or whether a key was stolen. [https://bscscan.com/tx/0xb5dccc1d7c4c4dd6ebdadb544e6bd21bc29852c56cc0aff22fcac30751f3c3b5][https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911]

Fusionist told users to pause transfers, trading and bridging on BNB Chain while it investigated. [https://x.com/fusionistio/status/2124586293812461578] Gate specified the spot-trade rollback window as 17:55 to 19:15 UTC on 22 November 2026 and scheduled perpetual settlement for 24 November. [https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911] It also switched that market to reduce-only. [https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911] Gate said affected deposits could be treated as fork tokens if Fusionist later deemed them invalid. [https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911] Neither notice explains who will bear users’ losses or announces a reimbursement plan. [https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911][https://x.com/fusionistio/status/2124586293812461578]

A token bridge holds reserve assets against tokens circulating on another chain. If that chain accepts unbacked tokens, a bridge that stays open can release real inventory against them. [https://explorer-endurance.fusionist.io/address/0xf3310e3f0D46FF5EE7daB69C73452D0ff3979Bed][https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911] Fusionist’s warning and Gate’s response establish an exploit and trading controls; the attacker’s route to privileged minting remains unresolved. [https://x.com/fusionistio/status/2124586293812461578][https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102911]

NEWS:

[NEWS] Pump.fun is ending external wallet sign-in and asking users to import keys.
Pump.fun’s reported notice says Phantom, Solflare and Ledger sign-in will end on 30 November 2026, and directs account holders to import their wallet key into Privy to retain access. [https://www.ethos.network/wire/story/1199] The notice’s wording, as reproduced by an open mirror of an X post, conflicts with terms that the post says promise never to ask for wallet private keys. [https://www.ethos.network/wire/story/1199] The available record is a repost of the notice, not a direct Pump.fun announcement, so the exact migration flow and key handling are unverified here. [https://www.ethos.network/wire/story/1199]

[NEWS] Chainflip sets a 23 November deadline for returning most stolen assets.
Chainflip Labs set 18:38 UTC on 23 November 2026 as the deadline for the vault attacker to return most of the assets and keep 10 percent as a bounty. [https://chainflip.io/return] The offer follows the 21 November drain of 122.86 bitcoin and assets from the Ethereum vault; the company’s page lists the amounts and the proposed return conditions. [https://chainflip.io/return] The page does not establish that funds were returned by the deadline, and no recovery is counted here without a confirmed transaction or company update. [https://chainflip.io/return]

[NEWS] Hong Kong publishes a bill to license additional virtual-asset services.
The Hong Kong government published a bill on 20 November 2026 covering businesses that deal in, advise on, manage or hold virtual assets for clients. [https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/20/P2026112000582p.htm] The proposal requires licensing or registration with the Securities and Futures Commission, including for banks offering covered services. [https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/20/P2026112000582p.htm] The government scheduled the bill’s first reading for 2 December 2026, so these requirements are proposed rather than in force. [https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/20/P2026112000582p.htm]

CULTURE:

[CULTURE] Kava voters weigh a pause on new lending.
Kava proposal 223 would set every Kava Lend borrowing cap to zero and restore a ten-dollar minimum debt, while allowing repayments to continue. [https://gov.1xp.com/] The proposal was still open on 23 November 2026, with the portal showing 70.2% turnout and all recorded voting power in favour at its 10:06 UTC update. [https://gov.1xp.com/] That tally is a live snapshot, not a final result, and the portal reports that some chain APIs were unavailable. [https://gov.1xp.com/]

[CULTURE] Pump.fun’s wallet migration has become a dispute about who should handle a key.
An open mirror reproduces a post warning that Pump.fun will end external wallet sign-in and ask users to import keys into Privy. [https://www.ethos.network/wire/story/1199] The post points to terms that it says promise never to request private keys, putting account continuity against the usual expectation that a wallet key stays with its holder. [https://www.ethos.network/wire/story/1199] Because the mirror carries a repost rather than a direct company statement, it does not establish the exact migration or key custody design. [https://www.ethos.network/wire/story/1199]
